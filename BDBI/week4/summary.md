# Week 4 - Data Analytics

## Focus

Use Power BI visuals and analytics features to answer business questions.

## Blackboard Material

- Lecture material
- Lab session

## Summary

This week moves from model building into interpretation. Dashboard visuals should support a business question, reveal a pattern, or justify a recommendation.

## Lab Checklist

- [ ] Create visuals for trends, categories, and comparisons.
- [ ] Add filters or slicers.
- [ ] Explain what each visual shows.
