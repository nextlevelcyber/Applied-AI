## Selection Criteria

- Can the dataset support at least three meaningful business questions?
- Can it support clear KPIs?
- Does it include enough categorical and numerical fields for useful visuals?
- Can it show trends, comparisons, or segmentation?
- Does it require manageable cleaning/modeling work?
- Can it support a recommendation, not only a description?
